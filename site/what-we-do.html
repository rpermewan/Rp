What we do

Source it. Pack it. Ship it. Deliver it.

Four connected capabilities that let one supplier handle everything — from a grower's farm gate to a mine-site mess on a Pacific island.

01 — Export & international supply

Exporting food, made simple.

From straight loads to mixed and consolidated containers, we design shipments around what our customers actually need — not what's easiest for us to pack. We hold active shipping relationships on Pacific lanes and run our own in-country importer in PNG to handle customs, quarantine and delivery.

  • Straight loads and mixed / consolidated container solutions
  • Reefer (temperature-controlled) and dry container management
  • Our own in-country importer in PNG — removes cross-border compliance for customers
  • Air freight for urgent or high-value perishables
  • Multi-destination Pacific routing, including emerging markets
  • Documentation: health certificates, phytosanitary, letters of credit, customs

02 — Procurement & sourcing

Whatever you need, we can usually source it.

We've built a highly diversified supply base that lets us quickly and efficiently source virtually any food product. Our roots in fresh produce give us speed and agility — we operate 24/7 and can adapt to new requirements more quickly than many traditional distributors.

  • Custom procurement and tender response for institutional buyers
  • Broad supplier network — Australian growers, premium frozen suppliers, global FMCG brands
  • Sourcing to catering specification — portion sizes, pack formats, shelf-life
  • Price benchmarking and competitive quoting for large-volume customers

03 — Supply chain & 3PL

Beyond buying and shipping.

We've developed supply chain integration capabilities that support customers with pick-and-pack and third-party logistics. Our unique proprietary software, built specially for Bungle and our customers, digitises order management, freight costing and container tracking — giving customers complete visibility.

  • Own packing operation — custom pack configurations for catering and export
  • 3PL services for other food exporters needing Pacific logistics support
  • order and freight tracking through our unique proprietary software
  • Container tracking and shipment visibility for full transparency

04 — Domestic fresh produce

Where we started — and still deliver daily.

Bungle retains its original fresh produce operations, with nationwide coverage and daily trade into retail and central markets across Australia. We supply major Australian retailers and run an active BungleFresh Care programme.

  • Premium citrus: Navels, Valencia, Blood Orange, Cara Cara, lemons, grapefruit
  • Table grapes, apples, easy-peelers and stonefruit
  • Major Australian retailer supply programmes
  • Growers Selection brand tiering — standard through premium
  • FRESHCARE and HARPS certified supply chain
