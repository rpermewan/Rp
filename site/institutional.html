Who we serve

Procurement with very high barriers to entry — that we already clear.

We supply government agencies, UN bodies, NGOs and institutional food programmes across PNG and the Pacific. We're registered on the UN Global Marketplace and hold the certifications, supply-chain infrastructure and in-country presence these programmes require. Our ability to deliver into complex, remote environments makes us well-suited to humanitarian and government food procurement.

  • UN Global Marketplace registration — positioned for UN and institutional procurement
  • HACCP, FRESHCARE, HARPS, SEDEX certified
  • Knowledge of Australian Government tender processes — AusTender, DFAT development procurement, Pacific aid programmes
  • Permanent in-country presence across PNG and the Pacific
  • Experience with complex, multi-destination container logistics

Cybersecurity & data protection

When you put us on your supplier list, you're trusting us with more than food.

Food businesses are now among the most-targeted sectors for ransomware — attacks rose 118% in late 2024 on the year before. When an institutional buyer shares tender documents, pricing and operational requirements, we treat that data with the same care we apply to our food. We take both cyber and physical security seriously, and we have the procedures to prove it.

Cyber security

Protecting your commercial information

  • Role-based access control across our proprietary software
  • Multi-factor authentication on all business systems
  • Tested data backup and recovery — ransomware cannot permanently destroy our data
  • Enterprise email security (Microsoft 365) and phishing-awareness training
  • Confidential handling of tender, pricing and government data under the Privacy Act 1988
Physical security

Across our sites and a complex region

  • Access-controlled facilities — visitor management and staff ID protocols
  • CCTV at our packing operations
  • Temperature monitoring and logging for cold-chain integrity
  • HACCP chain-of-custody and sealed-container protocols
  • Documented high-risk travel policy for PNG and Pacific operations
