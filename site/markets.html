01 — Papua New Guinea & the Pacific

Pacific-embedded, not Pacific-occasional.

PNG and the Pacific is a region we know deeply — a market we're strong in, though far from our only one. We've operated here for over 15 years and established a dedicated in-country import entity in Boroko, National Capital District. We have people on the ground, established carrier relationships, and deep knowledge of import regulations, quarantine and operational realities.

Our reach spans major centres, provincial towns and remote island locations — built with strong support from in-country government and trade partners. It's a depth of local network very few Australian exporters can match.

18 years. One market. Earned, not assumed.

  • Dedicated in-country import entity — Boroko, NCD
  • Customs, quarantine and in-country delivery handled end to end
  • Reach across major centres, provincial towns and remote islands
  • Full range — fresh produce, frozen and grocery
  • Expanding into the Solomon Islands (mining camp catering)

02 — Mine site catering

Among the most demanding food customers in the world.

Mine sites require FIFO-cycle consistency, strict specification compliance, full cold-chain integrity, and the ability to supply remote island or highland locations that are off the standard logistics map. Bungle has supplied mine-site catering across multiple PNG locations, including island and remote sites. We understand the tender process, the procurement intelligence required to win it, and the operational reality of delivering.

  • Full product range — fresh produce through frozen meals, dairy and dry goods
  • Compliance with mine-site health, safety and food-safety requirements
  • HACCP-certified supply chain
  • Pricing and supply to exact catering specifications
  • Island and remote delivery — reaching locations many suppliers can't

03 — Government & institutional

Built for procurement that has very high barriers to entry.

Bungle Group is positioned to supply government agencies, UN bodies and institutional food programmes across the Pacific. We're registered on the UN Global Marketplace and hold the certifications, supply-chain infrastructure and Pacific presence these programmes require. Our Pacific-embedded model and ability to deliver into complex environments make us well-suited to humanitarian and government food procurement.

  • UN Global Marketplace registration — positioned for UN and institutional procurement
  • HACCP, FRESHCARE, HARPS, SEDEX certified
  • Permanent in-country PNG presence
  • Experience with complex, multi-destination container logistics
  • Knowledge of Australian Government tender processes — AusTender, DFAT development procurement, Pacific aid programmes

Note: UN and WFP tenders are primarily closed processes. Bungle is registered, qualified and actively pursuing — honest and forward-leaning.

04 — Airline catering

Precision, consistency, reliable supply.

Bungle Group supplies fresh produce and food products into airline catering supply chains, and has been engaged in vendor-qualification processes with major international in-flight catering operators. Airline catering demands precise specification, consistent quality and dependable supply — our produce expertise and 24/7 operational model make us well-suited to these exacting requirements.

05 — Hotels, hospitality & retail

From the smallest island resort to large hotel groups.

We serve hotels, restaurants and retail customers across PNG, the Pacific Islands and throughout Asia — a single, reliable food supply partner wherever they operate. Our product range and logistics capability mean hospitality buyers can consolidate fresh produce and grocery/FMCG procurement through one relationship and one mixed container.

  • Fresh produce and major international FMCG brands in a single supply relationship
  • Mixed-container capability for consolidated procurement
  • In-country channels: catering, hotels and retail

06 — Australian retail

We've shown up every week for over a decade and a half.

Bungle has been an approved supplier to Australia's major grocery retailers for over 15 years — in one of the world's most demanding fresh produce supply chains: weekly tendering, strict specification, zero margin for error. We hold FRESHCARE, HARPS, SEDEX and HACCP, and participate in major-retailer RPC citrus programmes.

A supplier that has operated through weather events, COVID, supply-chain disruption and ACCC inquiries — and kept delivering — is a supplier you can rely on.
