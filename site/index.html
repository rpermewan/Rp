Est. 2008 — Australian food export & supply chain

Premium Australian fresh produce — citrus, grapes and stonefruit — supplied to Australian retailers and exported to the Pacific and Asia since 2008.

Bungle Group is foremost a fresh produce business. We market and export premium Australian citrus, table grapes, stonefruit and vegetables to retailers across Australia and internationally — and have grown to supply frozen food, dairy and grocery products to customers across Papua New Guinea, the Pacific Islands, Asia and the Middle East. Family-owned and operating 24 hours a day, seven days a week.

What we do

Fresh produce at the core. Everything else built around it.

Fresh produce underpins everything we do. We supply Australian retailers daily, export citrus, grapes and stonefruit to markets across Asia and the Pacific, and have extended our range to frozen food, dairy and grocery to meet the wider needs of our export customers — all managed with the speed and discipline of a fresh produce operation.

01 / Export

Export & international supply

Straight loads, mixed containers, reefer and dry — routed across the Pacific with our own in-country importer in PNG handling customs and delivery.

02 / Source

Procurement & sourcing

An established supply base across all major food categories, with the ability to respond to catering specifications, procurement schedules and institutional tender requirements.

03 / Move

Supply chain & 3PL

Our own packing operation, pick-and-pack and third-party logistics — with order and container tracking through our unique proprietary software.

04 / Grow

Domestic fresh produce

Our heartland. Premium Australian citrus and table grapes supplied to Australian major retailers daily. Navels, Valencias, Cara Cara, Crimson Seedless, Red Globe — the varieties that built Bungle.
