What we do

One supplier. Fresh, frozen, dairy, grocery — done.

We source, pack, ship and deliver food to some of the world's hardest-to-reach tables — managing the whole supply chain from Australian growers and processors through to in-country delivery.

01 / Export

Export & international supply

Straight loads, mixed containers, reefer and dry — routed across the Pacific with our own in-country importer in PNG handling customs and delivery.

02 / Source

Procurement & sourcing

A diversified supply base lets us source virtually any food product to catering specification — fast. Roots in fresh produce mean we move at produce speed.

03 / Move

Supply chain & 3PL

Our own packing operation, pick-and-pack and third-party logistics — with order and container tracking through our unique proprietary software.

04 / Grow

Domestic fresh produce

Our original business — premium Australian citrus, grapes, apples and stonefruit supplied daily into major retail and central markets nationwide.

Why Bungle

We don't just sell food — we solve the supply-chain problems that make hard markets hard.

We've built what hard markets actually require: our own in-country importer in PNG, our own packing operation, 3PL capability and established relationships with the Pacific's major shipping lines. We move at the speed of a fresh-produce business — 24/7 — across our entire range.
