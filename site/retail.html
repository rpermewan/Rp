Fruit quality

What major retailers require — and how we meet it.

Supplying fruit to Australia's major retailers means meeting detailed, constantly evolving quality standards — assessed at the orchard, in the packing shed, and again at the retailer's distribution centre. These are the things that matter most, and how we deliver them, week in and week out.

01 / Eating quality

Maturity & internal quality

Brix (sugar), acidity and the Brix-to-acid ratio decide whether fruit actually tastes good. We test maturity before every season — sliced-fruit samples and documented minimum and maximum Brix readings — so eating quality is right from the first delivery.

02 / Sizing

Size & grading

Retailers buy to precise size and count specifications. We grade to those ranges and hold them consistently — so the carton matches the spec, and the spec matches the shelf.

03 / Appearance

Defects & presentation

External quality is judged against defined major and minor defect tolerances — blemish, marking, shape and colour. Our grading is built around those tolerances to keep rejections to a minimum.

04 / Standards

Quality attribute standards

Each retailer maintains its own quality attribute standards and reviews them continually. We work hand-in-hand with retail quality teams to meet — and stay ahead of — these evolving specifications.

05 / Freshness

Cold chain & shelf life

Fruit is perishable and date-sensitive. Temperature-controlled handling and disciplined despatch protect condition and shelf life all the way to the distribution centre.

06 / Traceability

Specs, labelling & traceability

Correct product specifications, tray labels, barcodes (GTIN) and lot traceability — accurate every time, so product flows through the DC without holds or queries.

What retailers require — and we deliver

Five things major retailers demand of every supplier.

Bungle meets all of them. Here's exactly how.

01 / Food safety

Certification, not promises

Every fresh-produce supplier must hold an approved standard. We hold FRESHCARE FSQ4.2 (GFSI-benchmarked) plus HARPS — required for packing produce into retail-branded packaging for Australia's major supermarkets.

02 / Sustainability & ethics

Audited, shareable evidence

Retailers increasingly require SEDEX/SMETA audit data and traceability to farm level. We hold SEDEX membership and FRESHCARE Environmental certification — giving retailers the ethical-sourcing evidence they need, without self-declaration.

03 / Traceability & digital

Lot traceability, end to end

Digital traceability is now a baseline requirement. Our unique proprietary software, built specially for Bungle and our customers, covers purchase orders, inventory and freight visibility — accurate fulfilment, specification compliance, and traceability from packing shed to store.

04 / Reliability

Reliability you can plan around

Retailers run on tight inventory with weekly tendering and penalties for non-supply. Our 15+ year track record — through weather, COVID and disruption — is the clearest proof there is that we deliver, every week.

05 / Retailer-specific

Programmes & packaging

Beyond the base standard, retailers run Supplier Excellence Programs and packaging schemes. We participate in major-retailer RPC (Returnable Plastic Crate) citrus programmes — a direct commitment to retailer infrastructure.

+ / The result

Retail-grade, proven

We know what retailers need because we've delivered it for years. The same retail-grade standards now underpin everything we supply to export, mining, government and institutional buyers.
