Bungle PNG

A permanent presence, not a passing trade.

PNG and the Pacific isn't a market we visit — it's part of who we are. The Bungle family has been woven into Melanesian life for five generations, with deep personal ties to Fiji and Papua New Guinea stretching back well over a century. That heritage is the foundation of how we work in the region today, and a commitment we hold seriously.

We've traded here for more than 15 years as our own in-country importer — not an outsider passing through, but a business with our own people on the ground, our own carrier and government relationships, and hard-won knowledge of import regulations, quarantine and the day-to-day operational realities. Being our own importer means we own the outcome, end to end — and we answer for it.

Our reach spans major centres, provincial towns and remote island communities, built on the trust of in-country government and trade partners. It's a depth of local network — and local commitment — that very few Australian exporters can match. We're not here for a season. We're here for the long haul.

Five generations in Melanesia. Here for the long haul.

Mine-site catering

Mine site catering — supply to remote and island locations.

Mine site catering operations require consistent, specification-compliant food supply delivered to locations that are logistically demanding. Bungle Group has experience supplying mine site catering operators in Papua New Guinea, including island and remote sites. We understand the tender process, the documentation requirements and the operational standards these contracts demand.

  • Full product range — fresh produce through frozen meals, dairy and dry goods
  • Compliance with mine-site health, safety and food-safety requirements
  • HACCP-certified supply chain
  • Pricing and supply to exact catering specifications
  • Island and remote delivery — reaching locations many suppliers can't

Airline catering

Precision, consistency, reliable supply.

Bungle Group supplies fresh produce and food products into airline catering supply chains, and has been engaged in vendor-qualification processes with major international in-flight catering operators. Airline catering demands precise specification, consistent quality and dependable supply — our produce expertise and 24/7 operational model make us well-suited to these exacting requirements.
