About Bungle Group

Eighteen years of delivering in difficult places.

Bungle Group is an Australian food export and supply chain business — founder-led, privately owned, and built to deliver where logistics get difficult.

Who we are

First and foremost, a fresh produce business.

Everything Bungle does starts with fresh produce. We began in 2008 turning around citrus supply chains — getting the right fruit, at the right quality, to the right place — and that grower-to-shelf discipline still runs through everything we do. In our early years we grew our own fruit and ran a juice factory in Mildura, building deep, hands-on expertise in agronomy and post-harvest science.

We're a science-led business at heart. Our background in agronomy, and our investment in technology, let us measure, manage and protect quality at every step — and they're how we keep pushing the business forward. Product quality assurance isn't a box we tick; it's the standard we hold every line to, every day.

Today that produce mindset extends across frozen food, dairy and grocery too — supplied to customers from Australian retail to right across the Pacific — but it has never left us. We're a young, ambitious team carrying a serious craft.

Our values

What we'll never compromise.

We're an export and supply-chain business, but we run on a simple set of principles — and they don't move.

01 / Honesty

Transparency & honesty

Transparency and honesty are the foundation of every relationship we hold — with growers, customers and partners alike. They are non-negotiable, and we will never compromise them, whatever the deal.

02 / Doing right

Right over easy

We do what's right, not simply what's most profitable. When a decision is better for our growers and our customers but harder for us, that's still the decision we make.

03 / The link

Growers and customers first

Our growers and our customers are the most important people in our business. We see ourselves as the vital link that joins the two — and we protect both ends of that relationship.

04 / Science

Quality, proven by science

Quality is assured, not assumed. Our roots in agronomy and our use of post-harvest science and technology let us measure — and stand behind — every quality claim we make.

The evolution

Bungle changed because our customers' markets did.

Founded in 2008 by Richard Permewan as BungleFresh — a fresh produce exporter and domestic marketer. Over 15+ years, as our customers moved into the world's most demanding operating environments, we expanded our offer to match.

2008
Founded as BungleFresh — fresh produce export and domestic marketing, with owned farms and a Mildura juice factory.
2011
Major Australian retail citrus supply relationship established.
Mid 2010s
PNG operations formalised; in-country import company established in Boroko, NCD. Mildura farms and juice factory sold to a local family in 2015 — focus shifts fully to export and distribution.
Growth period
Product range extended into frozen food, dairy and FMCG to serve catering and mining customers.
Last 18 months
Added our own packing operation — deepening supply chain integration.
2025 / 2026
Operating under the Bungle Group brand with new proprietary software built specially for Bungle and our customers; UN Global Marketplace registered; positioned for government and institutional procurement across the Pacific.
// founder-led since 2008 Richard Permewan — Founder, Bungle Group

Founder-led

The people who run this business have their name on it.

Richard Permewan founded Bungle in 2008, building it from a single produce export venture into a multi-market food supply operation with staff across Australia and PNG. The business operates across several related entities — Bungle Group (export & supply chain), I Am Thirsty (dehydrated fruit and cocktail garnish), and Vantage Pacific (insurance broking for Pacific trade).

We are privately owned. No private equity pressure to cut corners, no offshore parent, no conflicting interests. In an industry where suppliers come and go, longevity is integrity made visible — our oldest customer relationships predate our youngest staff members.
