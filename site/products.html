Citrus is our heartland.

Citrus is the foundation of Bungle's produce expertise. Our growers

A fresh produce business — built alongside our growers.

Fresh produce is where Bungle began, and it remains the heart of who we are. We wouldn't be where we are today without our growers — partners we've grown alongside and walked this path with, season after season, hand in hand.

Our teams are constantly on the road through Australia's growing regions, and our office sits in Mildura, in the heart of the nation's food bowl. That keeps us present on the ground — close to the fruit, the farm and the season — working every day for both our customers and our growers.

Our role is a simple one: put the right fruit in the hands of the right customer. The best price point for the customer, and the best return for the grower. We sit between the two, and we look after both.

  • Teams constantly travelling Australia's growing regions
  • Office strategically placed in Mildura — the heart of the food bowl
  • Long-term grower partnerships, built season after season
  • Right fruit, right customer — best price for the buyer, best return for the grower

Specialist — fruit exports

Fresh fruit export is where Bungle started — and where our reputation was built.

Since 2008 we've specialised in marketing and exporting premium Australian fruit — with particular depth in citrus, table grapes, apples and stonefruit — to markets across the Pacific, Asia, Southeast Asia and the Middle East. Our exports are underpinned by agronomic expertise and post-harvest science: we know what premium fruit looks like at origin, and what it has to look like when it arrives at a hotel kitchen in PNG or a mining camp mess on a Pacific island.

Sea freight that makes long-haul export viable

The common misconception is that sea freight only works for the hardiest produce. We've built our export model around proving that wrong. With proper post-harvest management, correct variety selection, precise pre-cooling and controlled-atmosphere reefer containers, premium Australian citrus, grapes and apples can be exported by sea without compromising quality on arrival — and the same container can carry your frozen proteins, FMCG staples and baking supplies.

  • Post-harvest management — variety selection, pre-cooling and packing for sea transit
  • Reefer container management on Pacific and Asian trade lanes
  • Mixed-container consolidation — fruit with frozen, dairy and dry goods
  • FRESHCARE-certified supply chain
  • Phytosanitary and health-certificate management
  • Air freight for premium or time-sensitive shipments
